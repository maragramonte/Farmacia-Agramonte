About us

At Farmàcia Agramonte we work every day so that our customers get the best service we can give them.

A passion for health

At Farmàcia Agramonte we have a passion for health, and that is why our aim is to give the best pharmaceutical advice with the most human and professional care possible. We support and advise you through each and every one of your questions and treatments.

The pharmacy is run by Zoila Agramonte Bucho, who holds a degree in Pharmacy from the University of Havana and is a qualified pharmacist and dietitian with more than thirty years of experience in the pharmaceutical sector.

A protected Modernista shop

Farmàcia Agramonte, formerly Farmàcia Joaquim Cases, is a Modernista shop protected as a Cultural Asset of Local Interest and listed as an emblematic business of great interest.

The interior as it stands today comes from a Modernista refurbishment of 1880, the period in which the Cases family created a compounded formula that became popular throughout Spain and even in the Americas, the “Solución Cases”, known for easing a wide range of ailments and pains.

Outside, the façade is dominated by a panelled wooden shopfront covering its whole surface. Inside, the Modernista finishes typical of the period: glass with acid-etched floral motifs, hydraulic mosaic flooring, and cabinetwork with curved lines and floral detail.

Those Modernista motifs sit alongside remains of medieval architecture, such as the stone load-bearing arch inside. It dates from the 13th century, one of those built to make flexible spaces possible for workshops, shops and the like.

Four centuries of apothecaries

The earliest historical records of the pharmacy date from 1600, and describe a place run by a long line of apothecaries.

  • 1600The earliest date on record.
  • Until 1747The Saurina family, the first to run it.
  • 1864 – 2014The Cases family, owners for a century and a half.
  • Since 2019Zoila Agramonte Bucho, the new licence holder.

In 2019 Zoila decided to bring out the immense historical and artistic heritage of the pharmacy and, at the same time, give it energy and a modern outlook.
