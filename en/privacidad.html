Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Translation pending review. This is a translation of the privacy policy in Spanish, which is the original version and the one that prevails in the event of any discrepancy.

This website has no forms, no user registration, no accounts and no online payment. We collect no personal data through it. The data we do handle reaches us by other means — the counter, the telephone, email or WhatsApp — and this page explains what we do with it.

There is a basket for noting down what you want to order, and it does not change the above: the list is saved only in your browser, it asks you for no details, and it does not reach us until you send us the message on WhatsApp yourself. It is explained in detail in the cookie policy.

There is no data protection officer, and we are not required to have one: a neighbourhood pharmacy does not process health data "on a large scale" in the sense of Article 37 of the GDPR. For any question about your data, write to that same address and the licence holder will reply.

What data we handle, and what for

  • Orders by WhatsApp, telephone or email. Your name, your telephone number and what you ask us for — including the prescription, if you send it to us. We use it solely to prepare the order and let you know when it is ready.
  • Dispensing at the pharmacy. The health data recorded on the prescription, and anything we note down for pharmacotherapeutic follow-up where appropriate. This processing is required by health regulations.
  • Invoicing. The tax details needed to issue an invoice when you ask for one.

On what legal basis

  • Your consent, when you write to us to place an order.
  • The performance of the contract of sale and dispensing.
  • Compliance with legal obligations relating to health, accounting and tax.
  • For health data, Article 9(2)(h) of the GDPR: it is necessary for the provision of health care by a professional bound by professional secrecy.

Health data: a word of warning

A prescription is health data, in the most protected category. WhatsApp messages travel with end-to-end encryption, but the conversation stays saved on your phone and on ours. If you would rather not send the prescription that way, bring it to the counter or call us: it is just as valid.

How long we keep it

Order conversations, for as long as needed to deal with them and a reasonable margin afterwards. Clinical and accounting records, for the periods set by health and tax regulations — five and six years respectively, as a general rule.

Who we share it with

Nobody, for commercial purposes. We never sell or pass on data for advertising. It is only disclosed where the law requires: to the health authorities and to the Servei Català de la Salut when invoicing prescriptions, and to the Spanish tax authority and our accountants for accounting obligations.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you access to your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, and request its portability. Where the processing rests on your consent — the order you place on WhatsApp, for example — you can withdraw it whenever you like and without giving any reason; withdrawing it does not affect what we had done before, which was lawful. Just write to farmacia.lallana@gmail.com or come into the pharmacy with a document that identifies you. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

Data that leaves Europe

There is only one case, and it is up to you: WhatsApp, which belongs to Meta Platforms Ireland. If you write to us there, your number and the content of the message — including the prescription, if you send it — pass through their service, with the international transfers that Meta declares in its own privacy policy and over which the pharmacy has no control whatsoever. Meta states that it relies for this on the mechanisms the GDPR requires; it is worth checking that in their policy, since it is theirs to answer for.

If you would rather avoid it, call us on the telephone or bring it to the counter: it is just as valid and it goes no further than here.

Visiting this website, by contrast, sends nothing anywhere. The page makes not a single request to anyone else's servers: even the fonts are served from our own hosting. They used to be requested from Google Fonts, which sent your IP address to Google simply because you opened the page; that no longer happens.

Nothing is decided by algorithm

We make no automated decisions about you and we build no profiles. There is no system that scores, classifies or decides anything: behind every order there is a person at the counter reading it.
