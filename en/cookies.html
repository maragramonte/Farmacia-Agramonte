Cookie policy

Last updated: October 2026

Translation pending review. This is a translation of the cookie policy in Spanish, which is the original version and the one that prevails in the event of any discrepancy.

This website uses no cookies

Neither its own nor anybody else's. There is no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no embedded social media buttons. There is no tracking JavaScript of any kind.

That is why you will not see the usual cookie banner when you arrive: there is nothing to consent to.

The only thing saved in your browser: your basket

If you use the "Add" button in the catalogue, the list you build up is saved in your own browser's local storage, so that you do not lose it when you move from page to page. It is the only piece of data this website keeps on your device, and it is worth knowing exactly what that means:

  • It does not leave your browser. It is not sent to any server, it does not travel to the pharmacy and we cannot see it. It stays on your device.
  • It does not identify you. It stores product names and quantities, nothing else: not your name, not your email, not any identifier. It cannot be used to follow you here or on any other website.
  • It only reaches us if you send it. When you press "Send the order", your WhatsApp opens with the message already written, and you send it yourself. If you do not send it, we have no record that you looked at anything.
  • You can delete it whenever you like. With the "Empty the basket" button, or by clearing this site's data from your browser settings.

This does not need your prior consent either, and that is not an oversight: Article 22(2) of the Spanish LSSI exempts storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you have asked for, and a shopping basket is the textbook example. Even so, we would rather explain it here, which is what this page is for.

Not a single third-party request

When you open this page, your browser asks nothing of anyone but us. No analytics, no embedded maps, no videos, no fonts from elsewhere: the letters you are reading are served from this very site, not from Google's servers.

They used to be requested from Google Fonts, and that was enough for your IP address to reach Google without you doing anything at all. That no longer happens. Nobody outside the pharmacy finds out that you came here.

When you leave this website

The WhatsApp buttons — and the one that sends the order — take you to somebody else's site. From that point on, WhatsApp's (Meta's) cookie and privacy policy applies, not ours.

The same goes for the link to the La República feature on the About us page: until you click it, nothing is requested from anyone, and if you do click it you move to their website and their terms.

How to control cookies in general

Although there is no need here, every browser lets you view, block and delete cookies from any site through its privacy settings. It is usually under Settings → Privacy and security.

Changes

If one day the website takes on analytics or any other tool that does use cookies, we will update this page and the corresponding consent banner will appear before anything is installed.
