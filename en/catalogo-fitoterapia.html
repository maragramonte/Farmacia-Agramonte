How to order

With “Add” you note down whatever you like in your basket, which stays saved in this browser and you can change whenever you want. When you are done, the basket writes the message for you and you send it to us on WhatsApp: we prepare it, confirm the price and you collect it at the counter.

Nothing is paid here and we do not ask you for any personal details: this is not an online shop, it is a way to order without having to type the products out one by one. The counter is also where we can advise you.
