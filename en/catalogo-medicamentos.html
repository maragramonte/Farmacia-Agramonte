Medicines are ordered, not bought online

The law does not allow prescription medicines to be advertised to the public, and those that need no prescription may only be advertised with mandatory warnings. That is why this page carries no catalogue: it would be advertising medicines, which is a different matter from showing you a moisturiser.

What you can do is order them. Send us the prescription or the name of what you need on WhatsApp or by phone, we prepare it and let you know when it is ready. Dispensing and checking the prescription happen at the counter, as the rules require and as it should be: that is where we can ask you whether you are taking anything else.

If a symptom worries you, see a doctor first. What we give here is pharmaceutical advice, not a diagnosis.
