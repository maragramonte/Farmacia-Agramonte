Use of this site

This website is informational: it presents the pharmacy, its services and its contact details. It is not an online shop, and no medicine is sold or dispensed from here.

The catalogue basket is not a purchase: it is there to note down products so that the order message writes itself. No contract is entered into on this website, nothing is charged, and there is no payment gateway. The order is agreed on WhatsApp or by phone, the price is confirmed when we reply, and payment and collection take place at the counter, where the prescription is checked whenever the medicine requires one.

The health content we publish is general advice from a pharmacist. It is not a substitute for a medical consultation or for a diagnosis. If a symptom worries you, you should see a doctor.
